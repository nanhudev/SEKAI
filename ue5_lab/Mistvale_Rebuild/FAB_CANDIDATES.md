## Selection gate

Select one primary town family, at most one complementary architecture family, one foliage family and one prop family. Test license entitlements, UE 5.8 import, Lumen/High and reduced scalability, scale, collision, material palette and silhouette before placement. The bridge can be separately sourced if neither architecture family supplies it. Listings with an AI-generated flag or only low-resolution source imagery require extra scrutiny.

## Intended palette

Warm plaster, deep brown wood, cool blue-grey stone, dark teal/slate roofs, clear green foliage and restrained gold guild accents. Clear late-morning lighting first; beauty lighting later. This palette is a design target, not a claim that downloaded assets already match it.
