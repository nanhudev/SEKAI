TWO THINGS THAT WILL BE CHECKED, SO GET THEM RIGHT

1. Alcove and Cantilever cost ZERO RUN. They are WIDTH features, not length features.
   A bay in a 40 degree face is cut sideways into the hill (alcove) or cantilevered
   sideways off it (deck). The path keeps the ground's grade ALONG its length. If you
   level them along the path instead, you consume run and force the neighbouring
   flight to about 46 degrees. Do not do that.

2. Every variant that is a WALK rather than a FLIGHT (Alcove, Cantilever, Landing,
   Edge, End) still gets a flat or gently-sloped collision proxy on the same terms -
   a plain box, rotation 0, scale 1, slope baked into mesh data, MV_Stone_Base, named
   <object name>_RAMP. The split between visual and collision is absolute.
